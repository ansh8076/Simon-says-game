Simon says game

How to play

01

Watch the sequence

The board lights up one pad at a time, in one of four colours. Pay close attention, it only plays once.

02

Repeat it back

Tap or click the pads in the exact order they lit up. There is no time limit, but the order has to match.

03

The sequence grows

Get it right, and one more step gets added to the pattern. Round two is two pads, round three is three, and so on.

04

One wrong tap ends the run

A single mistake stops the game. Whatever round you reached becomes your score for that attempt.

05

Chase your best score

Every attempt is a fresh shot at a longer sequence. Try to beat your personal best each time you play.

Why it is worth playing

Sharpens working memory

Holding a growing sequence in your head and recalling it in order is a direct workout for short term memory.

Trains focus and reaction time

You have to watch closely and respond accurately, which builds concentration and quick, clean decision making.

A quiet, low pressure break

No timer, no opponent, just you against your own last round. A good way to reset between study or work sessions.

Genuinely replayable

Since the pattern is different every time, no two rounds feel the same, which keeps it interesting after the tenth try.

This game was built from scratch as a frontend project, piece by piece, from the colour logic to the sequence timing. If you catch a bug or think of a feature that would make it better, that feedback is genuinely welcome.

Now go ahead and see how long a sequence you can hold in your head.

Good luck, and have fun playing.
